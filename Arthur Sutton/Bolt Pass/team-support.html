Support for Bolt Pass

Bolt Pass is a free iOS app by Arthur Sutton. Email arthur.sutton85@126.com.

Use it to tap the opposite bolt on a flange, not the neighbor. The next bolt jumps across the circle. Counts, snug marks, and wrench names stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This sketch is not a substitute for the joint procedure. If a star looks wrong, write to arthur.sutton85@126.com with the flange name and the count you entered.
