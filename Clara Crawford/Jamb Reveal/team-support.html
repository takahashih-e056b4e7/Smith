Support for Jamb Reveal

Jamb Reveal is a free iOS app by Clara Crawford. Email clara.crawford66@126.com.

A hung door has four reveals: hinge head, hinge sill, strike head, and strike sill. Each one is counted in eighths of an inch, from 0 to 8. A zero means the door binds at that stop. When the widest and the narrowest differ by one or less, the sentence says the reveal is even. Otherwise it points at the widest stop and asks you to close it.

The app does not count hinges and it does not size the opening. Each door stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: clara.crawford66@126.com.
