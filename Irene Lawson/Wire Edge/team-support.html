Support for Wire Edge

Wire Edge is a free iOS app by Irene Lawson. Email irene.lawson74@163.com.

A chisel iron has a burr on one face. Hone the other face. Honing the face that already holds the burr leaves it there. When neither face holds a burr, the edge is keen.

Each iron stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: irene.lawson74@163.com.
