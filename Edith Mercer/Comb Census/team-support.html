The Application

Comb Census lets you paint how much of each side of each frame is covered by bees, open brood, capped brood, honey, and pollen, and works out colony strength, brood cells, stored honey, an emergence forecast, and a winter feeding plan from those marks. Coverage is judged by eye and colonies vary, so these numbers are estimates to support, not replace, your own judgement at the hive. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases.
