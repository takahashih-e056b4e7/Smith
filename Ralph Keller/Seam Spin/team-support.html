Support for Seam Spin

Seam Spin is a free iOS app by Ralph Keller. Email ralph.keller53@163.com.

Press the one seam a quilt block names. A crossing lies flat when its four seams already chase one way. Four Patch names the top seam to the right. Flat Block stays flat. Nine Patch names crossing 1-1. Pressing another seam does not move it.

Blocks stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: ralph.keller53@163.com.
