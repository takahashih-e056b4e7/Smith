Support for Camber Board

Camber Board is a free iOS app by Alice Ramsey. Email alice.ramsey35@sina.com.

Use it to drag a station along a beam chord. The required ordinate follows the curve, and the band says whether to heat, shim, or hold. Numbers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This sketch is not a substitute for the shop drawing. If an ordinate looks wrong, write to alice.ramsey35@sina.com with the span, midspan camber, and station you entered.
