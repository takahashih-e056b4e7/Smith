Support for Shaft Enter

Shaft Enter is a free iOS app by Louis Zimmerman. Email louis.zimmerman79@163.com.

Thread a four-shaft 2/2 twill. The longest float names one warp end, and Enter moves that end onto the shaft that breaks the float. The first end and the last end stay put. Drag that end when you want the same change by hand. Add ends when a warp is too short to judge.

Looms and threadings stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: louis.zimmerman79@163.com.
