The Application

Insert Life keeps one card for an insert, a grade, and a work material. You time a few life tests at different cutting speeds, log flank wear, and the app fits the Taylor line. It shows the lowest-cost speed and the speed that makes the most parts per hour. A photo of the edge can be marked to read VB. One machine turret holds the cards, and a shift plan groups tool changes into fewer stops, with a local reminder before each stop. A photo reading is an estimate. The app does not connect to the machine, does not read G-code, and does not replace the cutter maker's data. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases.
