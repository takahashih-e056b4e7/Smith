Support for Float Gap

Float Gap is a free iOS app by Eugene Zimmerman. Email eugene.zimmerman26@sina.com.

A floating floor has four expansion gaps: left, right, head, and foot. Each one is counted in eighths of an inch, from 0 to 8. Two, three, or four can stay. A turn toward one edge shortens it and lengthens the opposite edge. At zero or eight, that turn stops.

The app does not count bundles and it does not measure the room. Each room stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: eugene.zimmerman26@sina.com.
