The Application

Stream Gauging lets you measure the discharge of a small stream with the velocity-area method: draw the cross section, add verticals with depth and a current meter, float, or direct velocity, and see each vertical's share of the flow. After three or more visits it fits a stage-discharge rating curve, so later you can read the staff gauge, log the stage, and see the flow it stands for. Discharge and rating figures come from your own field measurements, so they support, and do not replace, your own judgement and any official records. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases.
