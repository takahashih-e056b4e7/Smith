Privacy Policy for Lap Splice

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Lap Splice is a free iOS app made by Margaret Vaughn, an individual developer. Contact: margaret.vaughn17@126.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app marks lap splices, tension zones, and stagger on beams you enter on this iPhone. Those beams stay on the phone. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Lap Splice does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics. Sharing a length uses the system share sheet, and only the text you choose to send leaves the phone.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
