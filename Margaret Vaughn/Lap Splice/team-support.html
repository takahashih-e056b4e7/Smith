Support for Lap Splice

Lap Splice is a free iOS app by Margaret Vaughn. Email margaret.vaughn17@126.com.

Use it to move a class B lap out of the tension zone and to see whether the next bar is staggered. Numbers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The sketch is not a substitute for the project drawings. If a length looks wrong, write to margaret.vaughn17@126.com with the bar diameter, cover, and clear span you entered.
