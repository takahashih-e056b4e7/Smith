Support for Closing Stave

Closing Stave is a free iOS app by Henry Tucker. Email henry.tucker96@126.com.

Extra width stays on the widest stave. Plane that stave until the croze closes. A short croze gets one new stave. Sharing the difference across the other staves does not close the croze.

Each cask stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: henry.tucker96@126.com.
