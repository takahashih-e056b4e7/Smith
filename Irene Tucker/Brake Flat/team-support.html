Support for Brake Flat

Brake Flat is a free iOS app by Irene Tucker. Email irene.tucker68@yahoo.com.

Use it to turn a flange chain into a flat length, check whether a return hits the press opening, and compare a trial coupon with the nominal flat. Numbers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

Tonnage is a reference. Prove the coupon in your shop. The result does not replace your press manual.

These terms apply to the free iOS app only. If a calculation looks wrong, write to irene.tucker68@yahoo.com with the flange lengths you entered.
