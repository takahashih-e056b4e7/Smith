Privacy Policy for Brake Flat

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Brake Flat is a free iOS app made by Irene Tucker, an individual developer. Contact: irene.tucker68@yahoo.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app calculates a flat pattern, bend order, and a reference tonnage from lengths and angles you type on this iPhone. Those parts stay on the phone. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Brake Flat does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics. Sharing a flat length uses the system share sheet, and only the text you choose to send leaves the phone.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
