Support for Mark Roll

Mark Roll is a free iOS app by Helen Dalton. Email helen.dalton48@outlook.com.

Use it to roll a spool until the match mark sits on the nearest bolt hole. The sentence tells you to roll back, roll clockwise, or set the bolts. Hole counts, the band, and clamp dogs stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This sketch is not a substitute for the bolt-up procedure. If a mark looks wrong, write to helen.dalton48@outlook.com with the hole count and the mark you entered.
