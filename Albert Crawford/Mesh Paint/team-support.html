Support for Mesh Paint

Mesh Paint is a free iOS app by Albert Crawford. Email albert.crawford50@gmail.com.

Tap the drive mark and the coast mark on a ring-gear tooth. The sentence tells you to move the pinion, to open or close the backlash, or that the mesh is centered. Each axle stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This is the face-hobbed reading used on the bench. It is not every tooth form, and it does not choose a shim thickness. Questions: albert.crawford50@gmail.com.
