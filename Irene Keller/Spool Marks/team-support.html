Support for Spool Marks

Spool Marks is a free iOS app by Irene Keller. Email irene.keller17@sina.com.

Use it to drag an outlet so both bend marks, the straight land, and the cut length move together, then decide whether to rotate the pipe or recut. Numbers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The sketch is not a substitute for the isometric. If a mark looks wrong, write to irene.keller17@sina.com with the rise, roll, and angle you entered.
