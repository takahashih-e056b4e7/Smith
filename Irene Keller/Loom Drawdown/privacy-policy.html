Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Loom Drawdown app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Irene Keller (collectively, the "Application"). Irene Keller is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect, log, or store any personal information when you download and use it. Registration is not required. Weaving drafts, color orders, yarns, and loom settings are saved on this iPhone. The Application does not use the camera, microphone, or location. When you choose to export a draft file, a swatch card, or a yarn list, the system share sheet sends it only where you pick. It does not require an account and has no in-app purchases. If the Application is used with an active internet connection, technical protocol data (such as your ephemeral IP address) is transmitted to facilitate network connectivity, but this data is not retained or used for tracking.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

No. The Application does not ask for this iPhone's location and does not read it.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Since the Application does not collect any information for the Service Provider, no data is shared with third parties. A share sheet delivers the machine name only to the destination you choose.

What are my opt-out rights?

Since the Application does not collect personal information through normal use, uninstalling it simply removes the Application from your device.

If you contact the Service Provider directly or voluntarily provide information by other means, you may request deletion of that information by contacting irene.keller17@sina.com.
