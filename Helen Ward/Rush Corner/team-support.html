Support for Rush Corner

Rush Corner is a free iOS app by Helen Ward. Email helen.ward23@gmail.com.

Rush Corner names the next corner of a rush seat. A wider rail is filled first. The hank is spliced when it is shorter than the next wrap. When a short rail is full, the sentence switches to crossing the open pair.

Chairs stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: helen.ward23@gmail.com.
