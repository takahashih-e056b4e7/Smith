Privacy Policy for Hex Clock

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Hex Clock is a free iOS app made by Harold Nolan, an individual developer. Contact: harold.nolan21@163.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the joints you clock on this iPhone: the name, the nominal size, how many flats you have turned, and whether the backup wrench is on. Those joints stay on the phone. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Hex Clock does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
