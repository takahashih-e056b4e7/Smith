Support for Shingle Offset

Shingle Offset is a free iOS app by Henry Vaughn. Email henry.vaughn74@outlook.com.

A three-tab shingle is 36 inches. The cut is the inches trimmed off the left end, from 0 to 18. Joints sit at 36 minus the cut, then every 36 inches, while they stay inside the slope. The course below is the starter, and its own cut starts at 6. The nearest gap is the smallest horizontal distance between a joint on this course and a joint on the starter. Four inches or more, and the sentence says this course is offset. Less than that, and it says which rake to shift toward.

The app does not place nails and it does not count bundles. Each slope stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: henry.vaughn74@outlook.com.
