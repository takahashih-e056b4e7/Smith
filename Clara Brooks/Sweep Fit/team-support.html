Support for Sweep Fit

Sweep Fit is a free iOS app by Clara Brooks. Email clara.brooks83@163.com.

Use it to drag the corner of a conduit run. The sentence tells you the sweep holds, to ease the bend, or that a leg is too short. The runs stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This sketch is not a substitute for the bending procedure. If a radius looks wrong, write to clara.brooks83@163.com with the diameter and the factor you entered.
