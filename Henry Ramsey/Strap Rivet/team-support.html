Support for Strap Rivet

Strap Rivet is a free iOS app by Henry Ramsey. Email henry.ramsey80@sina.com.

Drag a rivet onto a leather strap. If it lands in the buckle fold, on an old hole, on the edge, or closer than the gap you set, that rivet goes back. Rivets already set stay where they are, so the gaps can be uneven.

Straps stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: henry.ramsey80@sina.com.
