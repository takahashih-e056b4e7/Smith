Privacy Policy for Strap Rivet

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Strap Rivet is a free iOS app made by Henry Ramsey, an individual developer. Contact: henry.ramsey80@sina.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the straps, folds, holes, and rivet positions you enter on this iPhone. A strap summary can be shared only when you tap Share. All of this stays on the phone unless you choose to share that summary. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Strap Rivet does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
