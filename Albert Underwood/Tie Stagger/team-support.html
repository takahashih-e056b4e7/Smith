Support for Tie Stagger

Tie Stagger is a free iOS app by Albert Underwood. Email albert.underwood52@163.com.

Tap the next crossing on a rebar mat. The sentence tells you to keep staggering, that the mat is staggered, that a bay is still open, or that the row or column beside it is already tied. Each mat stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

The ratio is the shop's own share for that mat. It is not a code table. This sketch is not a substitute for the tying procedure. Questions: albert.underwood52@163.com.
