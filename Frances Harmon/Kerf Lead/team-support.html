Support for Kerf Lead

Kerf Lead is a free iOS app by Frances Harmon. Email frances.harmon61@yahoo.com.

Use it to drag a lead-in along a plate edge. The kerf, tab, and straightening allowance move together. Flip keeps the tab. Recut after the tab has moved removes it. Numbers stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This sketch is not a substitute for the cut procedure. If a tab looks wrong, write to frances.harmon61@yahoo.com with the edge length and the lead you entered.
