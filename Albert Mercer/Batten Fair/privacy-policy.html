Privacy Policy for Batten Fair

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Batten Fair is a free iOS app made by Albert Mercer, an individual developer. Contact: albert.mercer20@163.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores the boats, waterlines and half-breadths you enter on this iPhone. A loft card can be shared only when you tap Share. All of this stays on the phone unless you choose to share that card. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Batten Fair does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.
