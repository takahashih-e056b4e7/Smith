Support for Batten Fair

Batten Fair is a free iOS app by Albert Mercer. Email albert.mercer20@163.com.

Enter a waterline as half-breadths on equally spaced stations. Third differences name the one station to ease, and Ease moves that station by at most one inch. The stem and the transom stay put. Drag that station when you want a smaller step. Add stations when a line is too short to judge.

Boats and half-breadths stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: albert.mercer20@163.com.
