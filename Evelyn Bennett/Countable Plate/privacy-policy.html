Privacy Policy for Countable Plate

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Countable Plate is a free iOS app made by Evelyn Bennett, an individual developer. Contact: evelyn.bennett85@163.com. Postal contact: United States.

The app stores each sample you add on this iPhone: the sample name, the dilution series, the plate photos you take or choose, the colonies you mark and the counting rules you save. The camera is used only when you tap Camera to photograph a plate. Photos you pick from your library are copied into the app and nowhere else. All of this stays on the phone. The app does not require an account, does not sell anything, and does not track you.

Countable Plate does not collect personal data, does not use advertising, and does not use third-party analytics.

The app is intended for a general audience. The age rating is 0.

Questions about this policy: evelyn.bennett85@163.com.
