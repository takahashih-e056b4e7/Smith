Support for Countable Plate

Countable Plate is a free iOS app by Evelyn Bennett. Email evelyn.bennett85@163.com.

Add a sample with its dilution series, then open each plate and tap the colonies on its photo. On a crowded plate, switch to Sector and count a half, quarter or eighth. The app keeps the dilutions the counting rule allows, following the plate count calculation in ISO 7218, and shows the result to two significant figures. Open the result to see every step. A qualified analyst should check each result before it is released.

Samples, photos and counts stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: evelyn.bennett85@163.com.
