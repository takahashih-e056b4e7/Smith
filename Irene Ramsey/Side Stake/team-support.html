Support for Side Stake

Side Stake is a free iOS app by Irene Ramsey. Email irene.ramsey93@outlook.com.

Side Stake names which spokes of a round basket take a second stake. A spoke holds at most two stakes. When the count would be even, one short bye-stake makes it odd. If a second stake is on the wrong spoke, the sentence names only the spoke to move.

Rounds stay on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy. Questions: irene.ramsey93@outlook.com.
