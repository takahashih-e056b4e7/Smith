Support for Even Take

Even Take is a free iOS app by Frances Quinn. Email frances.quinn49@126.com.

Turn the barrel and both ends of the turnbuckle move together. Turn one end and the other stays. The sentence says an end is too deep, that you should take an end in, that the ends should match, or that both ends match. Each line stays on this iPhone. There is no account and nothing to buy.

This follows the published rule that the exposed threads on the two ends are equal and not more than three. It does not measure tension and it does not count broken wires. Questions: frances.quinn49@126.com.
